In the Aisle


2019
Photo Series, Wheat pasting and Zine Installation


















In the Aisle gathers snippets of conversation and textures scavenged from Chinese supermarkets in Edinburgh. These supermarkets function as a third space, playing vital social roles within the Chinese diasporic community.

Driven by a search for social and emotional familiarity, the work finds comfort in the sights and smells that echo home. In these spaces, even the most humble and banal conversations give rhythm to daily life, while their scents and scenes ease the anxieties of being in a foreign land.

In the Aisle was made during a 14-day study trip to Edinburgh that was organised by NTU School of Art, Design and Media. The work was selected as part of a student showcase at the Pingyao International Photography Festival 2019, China (Photo credits: Fitri Yaccob) and shown in Bearings at the ADM Gallery, Singapore (Photo credits: Lin Shiau Yu).
