Hong Shu-ying 方舒颖 hoards images and stories as a way to make sense of how we learn and remember. She is fascinated by how systems of circulation and copying are often imperfect and sometimes contradictory.

She grew up in amateur Chinese orchestras where she learnt through emulating and copying, surrounded by hand-copied and photocopied scores. As a teen, she was photocopying scores and reposting images on Tumblr. This evolved into a particular way of looking at found materials, observing how meaning drifts through repetition and translation. Whether it’s scores and annotations, newspapers, or pictures of the skies, her curiosity remains focused on what persists, shifts, or disappears as material moves across contexts and time.

Working across moving images and printed matters,, she is interested not only in what people collect, but how they organise and share knowledge. To expand how art books are circulated and accessed, she co-founded Part Time Book Club, an informal collective organising gatherings, exchanges, and showcases around art books. Frustrated by limited access to formal residencies and the demanding logistics of international travel, she also conceived | | on paper. The residency is anchored in conversations and four sheets of A4 paper, and grounded in the belief that resource-lean artist initiatives can also create meaningful spaces for dialogue and iteration.

Hong Shu-ying received the Kwek Leng Joo Prize of Excellence in Photography and was featured in the 6th Women in Film and Photography Showcase by Objectifs Centre for Photography and Film (Singapore). She has presented solo exhibitions at Esplanade (Singapore, 2024) and Yao Alternative Space 窯座 (Taichung, 2024), with an upcoming presentation at RM Gallery (Auckland, 2026). Her work has also been shown at Three Shadows Xiamen Photography Art Centre and Pingyao International Photography Festival (China); Jakarta International Photography Festival (Indonesia); PRINTROOM (Netherlands); CICA Museum (South Korea); Pon Ding 朋丁 (Taiwan); and DECK, I_S_L_A_N_D_S Peninsula, Art Outreach, and ADM Gallery (Singapore). She holds a BFA (Hons) in Media Art (Photography) from the School of Art, Design and Media, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

She is currently a resident at the Singapore Art Museum.
