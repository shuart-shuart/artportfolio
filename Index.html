Hong Shu-ying 方舒颖 reframes and reencounters traces left by others and by time. Working primarily with books, moving images, and printed matter, she explores how knowledge and memory take shape and find continuity across times and spaces.

Her practice attends to amateur practices and everyday gestures that leave unintentional records. This is rooted in her formative years in amateur Chinese orchestras and an early immersion in internet culture, where learning unfolds informally and collectively. Drawing from handwritten scores and annotations, found footage, and circulated images, she considers how acts of sharing, copying, and repetition produce forms of collective memory.

Hong also initiates collaborative projects that expand how art and art books are encountered. She co-founded Part Time Book Club, a collective that organises irregular gatherings to share and discover art books. In parallel, she runs |      | on paper, an alternative residency that proposes a more resource-lean model for artistic exchange — unfolding in iterations concurrent with her own residencies.

Hong Shu-ying received the Kwek Leng Joo Prize of Excellence in Photography and was featured in the 6th Women in Film and Photography Showcase by Objectifs Centre for Photography and Film (Singapore). She has presented solo exhibitions at Esplanade (Singapore, 2024) and Yao Alternative Space 窯座 (Taichung, 2024), with an upcoming presentation at RM Gallery (Auckland, 2026). Her work has also been shown at Three Shadows Xiamen Photography Art Centre and Pingyao International Photography Festival (China); Jakarta International Photography Festival (Indonesia); PRINTROOM (Netherlands); CICA Museum (South Korea); Pon Ding 朋丁 (Taiwan); and DECK, I_S_L_A_N_D_S Peninsula, Art Outreach, and ADM Gallery (Singapore). She holds a BFA (Hons) in Media Art (Photography) from the School of Art, Design and Media, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.
